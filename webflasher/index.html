Erase entire flash?

This permanently erases all 4 MB of flash on the ESP32-S3: the firmware, Wi‑Fi credentials, saved presets and settings.

The sign will not run until you flash firmware again (choose Full install).

Before you start

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer (Web Serial is not available in Firefox or Safari).
  2. Plug the sign's ESP32-S3-Zero into your computer with a USB-C data cable (the cable that powers the sign works, if it carries data).
  3. Click Connect and pick the USB device for the ESP32-S3 (it is usually listed as USB JTAG/serial debug unit or USB Serial).
  4. If no port shows up, or Connect fails: unplug the cable, hold the BOOT button on the board, plug it back in (or tap RESET while holding BOOT), release BOOT, then click Connect again.
  5. Click Install. Don't unplug until it says Flash complete.

Choose Full install the first time, after Erase flash, or when you are unsure. It writes the bootloader, partition table and firmware (at 0x0), and it clears Wi‑Fi credentials, presets and settings. Update app only writes just the firmware (at 0x10000) and keeps your Wi‑Fi, presets and settings, but it only works on a board that already ran a full install.

After flashing, the sign restarts and its USB port re-appears, so click Connect again if you want to flash another time. Its web portal then opens at http://semaphoresign.local (or join the SEMAPHORESIGN-XXXX Wi‑Fi network, password SEMAPHORESIGN, if it has no home Wi‑Fi saved).
